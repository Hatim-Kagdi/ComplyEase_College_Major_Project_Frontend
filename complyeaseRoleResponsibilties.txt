Advance Notification Module
CA + Admin Notification Flows You Should Add

1. CA Registration Request (Done)
When a new CA registers:
Admin should receive:
“New CA approval request”
CA should receive:
“Your account is pending approval”

This is a real onboarding workflow.

2. CA Approval(Done)
When admin approves CA:
CA should receive:
approval success email
Example:
Your CA account has been approved.
You can now log in to ComplyEase.

3. CA Rejection (Optional but Professional)
If later you add reject functionality
CA receives:
Your CA verification request was rejected.
Very real-world feature.

4. User Requests CA (Done)
When business owner requests CA assignment:
Admin receives:
Business XYZ requested CA assignment.

5. CA Assigned To Business (Done)
This is a BIG workflow.
When admin assigns CA:
User receives:
CA Rahul Sharma has been assigned to your business.
CA receives:
You have been assigned to business ABC Pvt Ltd.
This makes the platform feel alive.

6. Compliance Status Updated By CA (Done)
When CA marks compliance completed:
User receives:
GST_RETURN compliance marked COMPLETED by your CA.
This is extremely useful.

7. Compliance Overdue (Done)
Scheduler can detect overdue compliances.
User receives:
Your GST filing is overdue.
Optional:
CA also receives.

8. Document Uploaded (Done)
When user uploads document:
Assigned CA receives:
New document uploaded for ABC Business.
Very realistic CA workflow.

9. Account Deactivated (Done)
If admin disables user:
User receives:
Your account has been temporarily deactivated.
