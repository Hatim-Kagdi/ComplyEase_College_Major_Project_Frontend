1. The "Document-Compliance" Link (Missing Core Logic) (Done)
Right now, you have Documents and you have Compliances as separate entities. In a real system, a document should belong to a specific compliance record.

The Gap: If I upload a "Bank Statement," the CA needs to know it is for "GST March 2024."

The Feature: When a user views a Compliance record, they should see an "Attached Documents" list.

Implementation: In your Compliance Entity (Java), add a List<Document> or simply allow the User to "link" an existing document ID to a compliance ID.

2. The "Comment/Query" Loop (Missing Communication)
Compliance is rarely a "one-click" success. Often, the CA looks at a document and finds a mistake.

The Gap: If the CA marks a status as "REJECTED" or "NEEDS REVIEW," the User doesn't know why.

The Feature: A Comment Section on each Compliance record.

The Workflow: 1. CA marks status as "REJECTED."
2. CA types: "The invoice for $500 is missing the GST number. Please re-upload."
3. User sees the comment and uploads the corrected file.

3. The "Deadline Countdown" (Missing Urgency)
Compliance is entirely date-driven. Missing a date by one hour can cost a business thousands in fines.

The Gap: Your dashboard shows "Upcoming Due Dates," but users are lazy—they need visual urgency.

The Feature: Priority Indicators.

Due in > 7 days: Green

Due in < 3 days: Yellow

Due Today/Overdue: Red (Blinking)

The Feature: A "Days Remaining" counter (e.g., "3 days left to file").

4. CA Portfolio Overview (Missing for CA Role)
A CA doesn't just manage one client; they manage 20-50.

The Gap: If your CA dashboard just lists every compliance for every client in one big table, it becomes a mess.

The Feature: Client-Wise Filtering.

The CA should be able to select "Business A" and see only their records, then switch to "Business B."

A summary card for the CA: "You have 5 clients with overdue compliances."
